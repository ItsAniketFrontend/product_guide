Adding a product, step by step

How to add a new stone design so its page looks and works like every other product on the site: the right category, the Size / Material / Colour choices, the colour textures, and the extra details such as FAQs. No code needed.

1. Create the product

  1. In the Shopify admin go to Products›Add product.

  2. Title

    The design name as customers should see it, for example Palm Scape Design.

  3. Description

    Paste the long product description. Use the editor's bold or heading buttons for small headings. Do not paste FAQs here; they have their own field (step 5) and show as an accordion.

  4. Price

    Leave the price at 0.00. The site never shows prices; every product is “Request a Quote”.

2. Put it in the right category

Categories on the site fill themselves from two places on the product: Type and Tags. They must be spelled exactly as in the table, including capital letters.

  1. Product type

    In the Product organization box, set Type to the sub-category, for example Nature Inspired Patterns. It appears above the product title and in the breadcrumb.

  2. Tags

    Add two tags: the main category and the sub-category. Example for a bamboo wall design:

    Decorative Wall CladdingNature Inspired Patterns

    Optional room tags put the product in the “By Space” menu: Space: Living Room, Space: Bedroom, Space: Bathroom, Space: Kitchen, Space: Office Room.

3. Add Size, Material and Colour

These are the choices customers click on the product page. On the site they appear in this order: Size, then Material, then Colour, and each colour shows its stone texture automatically.

  1. In the Variants box click Add options like size or color.

  2. Option names

    Create up to three options, named exactly Size, Material and Colour (British spelling, with a “u”). Skip any the product does not have; a basin with one stone might only need Size.

  3. Option values

    Type the values exactly as listed in the reference, for example 2 M X 3 M, Customized Size, Natural Indian Sand Stone, White Marble. A colour spelled differently will show a plain tile instead of its texture.

  4. Remove combinations that do not exist

    Shopify creates every combination, including ones like Sand Stone + White Marble. Delete those variants (tick them, then Delete variants). The site only offers the colours that exist for the material the customer picks, so a marble colour will never show under sandstone if its variant is gone.

  5. SKU

    Give every variant the product's SKU, for example SABS000620. It is shown on the page as “SKU”.

Tip: the quickest way to set up a new design is to open a similar product and use More actions › Duplicate. The copy keeps its options, tags, type and details; change the title, description, photos and SKU.

4. Add photos

  1. Order matters

    • The first photo is the main image on the product page and on category pages.
    • The second photo appears when a customer hovers over the product on a category page, and as the wide image further down the product page.
    • Drag photos to reorder them.

  2. Photos for a colour

    To make a photo appear when a customer picks a colour, click the photo, choose Add alt text and include the colour name, for example Bamboo Design - Grey Marble. You can also link a photo to its variants in the Variants box.

5. Fill in the product details

Scroll to the bottom of the product page in the admin to the box called Metafields. These fields are optional; anything left empty is simply not shown.

Shipping text example

Production Timeline
This depends on the quantity of stone work. But for example, for a typical 3m X 3m wall, the production time is around 60 days.

Shipping Timeline
Shipping timelines are as per actuals. Typically, it takes around 3 weeks end-to-end, to deliver goods from India to UAE or other countries in GCC.

FAQ example

Type into the FAQ (questions & answers) field. Start each question with Q: and its answer with A:, and leave an empty line between questions. No quotes or brackets needed.

Q: How do I maintain this wall?
A: Wipe with a soft, damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals.

Q: Can it be used outdoors?
A: Yes, it is suitable for outdoor use.

Leave it empty for products that have no FAQs; then no FAQ section is shown. The older FAQ (accordion, JSON) field still works, including schema.org FAQPage JSON from SEO tools, but the plain-text field above is easier. Remember to click Done and then Save on the product.

6. Publish and check

  1. Set Status to Active, and under Publishing make sure Online Store is ticked. Click Save.

  2. Click View at the top of the product and check:

    • The breadcrumb reads Home › Collections › your category › your sub-category.
    • Size, Material and Colour appear in that order, and every colour shows a stone texture, not a plain beige tile.
    • Picking a material shows only that material's colours.
    • The product appears in its category and sub-category pages.

7. Adding a colour that is not in the list yet

The 17 colours in the reference already have their textures. For a brand-new colour:

  1. Go to Online Store›Themes and click Customize on the live theme.

  2. In the page selector at the top, choose Products › Default product.

  3. In the left panel, under the SKL Product section, click Add block › Colour swatch.

  4. Type the Colour name exactly as you will use it in the products' Colour option, choose the Texture picture (a square photo of the stone works best), then click Save.

Every product with that colour now shows the new texture. You can also use a Colour swatch block to replace the picture of an existing colour.
